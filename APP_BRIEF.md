## 🧠 What Your App Is (In Simple Human Words)

IntelliMeetPro is an AI-powered meeting assistant that automatically joins your online meetings, records conversations, transcribes them, and turns them into clean summaries, key points, and actionable tasks — without you needing to take notes.

---

## ⚡ What Problem It Solves

People waste time during and after meetings trying to:

* Take notes while listening
* Remember key decisions
* Extract action items
* Revisit long recordings

Important details get missed, and productivity drops.

IntelliMeetPro eliminates this by:

* Automatically capturing everything said
* Converting conversations into structured insights
* Making meetings searchable and actionable

* Triggers bot scheduling without manual input

### 🤖 Automated Meeting Bot

* Joins meetings on:

  * Zoom
  * Google Meet
  * Microsoft Teams

### 🎧 Automatic Audio Capture

* Captures meeting audio via bot
* No manual recording required
* Secure and session-based handling

### 📝 AI Transcription (WhisperX)

* Converts audio into highly accurate text
* Speaker-aware transcript (if supported)
* Timestamped segments for future use

### 🧠 AI Meeting Intelligence (LLM Processing)

  * **Action Items** → clear tasks extracted from discussion

### 🗄️ Meeting Data Storage
